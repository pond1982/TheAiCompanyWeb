Frequently asked questions

How do I save my game?

It saves automatically. Your company is stored locally on your device and restored when you reopen the app — just tap Continue.

How do I start? What do I do first?

Tap Start, pick a founder, then use the Build button to pitch your first product. Choose a Modality and Application, ship it, and see how the critics react. From there: hire a team, raise funding, and grow.

The game pauses when I have no product — is that a bug?

No. When you're idle and low on cash, the clock pauses as a safety net so you don't go bankrupt by accident. Pitch a new product or raise funding to continue.

How do I unlock the "god-tier" engineers?

They're tied to secret achievements — ship a near-flawless product, beat your rival enough times head-to-head, or reach a billion-dollar valuation. Your in-game assistant, Cora, drops hints as you get close.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

None. The game is completely free, with no ads, no purchases, and no accounts.

Does the game collect my data?

No. It makes no network requests and collects nothing — see our Privacy Policy. Everything stays on your device.

How do I turn the music or sound off?

Open the ⓘ (Credits) screen from the top bar and toggle Music & sound. The game also respects your device's silent switch.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Please email us with your device model and iOS version and a quick description — it really helps. Thank you!
